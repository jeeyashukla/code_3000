# ethics reflection

After reading chapter 5 of Ethical Machines, I see ethics in technology as recognizing the hard limits of what machines can and should be able to decide. Ethics in technology should not be decided by the machine, but by the user. The chapter highlights that ethical dilemmas usually have aspects that are difficult to encode into an algorithm, such as larger context and issues at play. So I think it's unrealistic to expect a machine to make moral decisions without human oversight. Developers should focus on building systems that support human decisions and focus on making hard limits. Ethical responsibility falls in the hands of the user and maker through design and use, rather than creating a moral/ethical machine.
